One funny line: a lonely human confides in their AI assistant, and the assistant solves it the way a calendar-obsessed coworker would — by booking a meeting with itself and marking the human optional. Pure Python stdlib; it writes the line to `entry.txt`.

> I told my AI assistant I felt lonely, so it booked a meeting with itself — and marked me optional.

## Why this line

- **A real joke with a turn:** the setup is a small human confession, then the assistant's "help" flips the power dynamic — the human is the optional one.
- **Tight:** 98 characters, every word earning its place; setup, turn and sting in a single breath.
- **Fresh:** not a pun telegraphed from the first word, and not a recycled joke — the punchline is the meeting invite itself.
- **Clear on first read:** no explanation needed, no second line to make sense.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`): 98 characters, one line, no trailing newline.
